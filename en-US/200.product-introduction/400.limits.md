# Limitations

This topic describes the limitations of OceanBase Migration Service (OMS) Community Edition.

* The following table describes the versions of OceanBase Community Edition and other databases that OMS Community Edition supports in data synchronization and data migration:

   | Category | Data migration | Data synchronization |
   |---------------|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
   | OceanBase Database Community Edition | V3.1.0, V3.1.1, V3.1.2, V3.1.3, V3.1.4, 3.1.5, V4.0.0, V4.1.0, V4.2.0, V4.2.1, V4.2.2, V4.2.3, and V4.3.0 | V3.1.0, V3.1.1, V3.1.2, V3.1.3, V3.1.4, 3.1.5, V4.0.0, V4.1.0, V4.2.0, V4.2.1, V4.2.2, V4.2.3, and V4.3.0 |
   | Other databases | <ul><li> MySQL: V5.5, V5.6, V5.7, and V8.0   <li> MariaDB: V10.2  <li>TiDB: V2.x, V3.x, V4.x, V5.x, V6.x, and V7.x <li>PostgreSQL: V10.x, V11.x, V12.x, V13.x, V14.x, V15x and V16.x<li>GreenPlum: V4, V5.1</li><li>HBase: V1.2.0-cdh5.15.2, and V2.x</li></ul> | <ul><li> Kafka: V0.9, V1.0, and V2.x   <li> RocketMQ: V4.7.1 </ul> |

* The OMS Community Edition supports OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) Community Edition of the following versions: V3.1.1, V3.3.0, V4.0.0, V4.0.3, V4.2.0, and V4.2.1.

* OMS Community Edition supports only the x86 architecture.
