# Manage alert settings

OceanBase Migration Service (OMS) allows you to configure the built-in monitoring metrics, alert thresholds, and alert frequency, and create, edit, and delete alert channels in the OMS console.

## Background information

OMS alerts generated upon monitoring metrics such as the long-term synchronization channel status are sent to the customer environment by using OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP). On the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Audit.constants.AlarmSettings">Alert Settings</UI-TERM>** page, you can configure the thresholds of different protection levels for data migration and synchronization tasks.

OMS supports the following alert protection levels: **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.WarnLevel.Unprotected">No Protection</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.WarnLevel.HighProtection">High Protection</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.WarnLevel.MediumProtection">Medium Protection</UI-TERM>**, and **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.WarnLevel.DiBaoHu">Low Protection</UI-TERM>**.

* No Protection: No alert is generated. This protection level is suitable for short-term migration or business scenarios with low real-time requirements.

* High Protection: This protection level is applicable to scenarios with extremely high requirements on latency.

  A high protection alert is triggered when:

  * The synchronization latency is greater than the specified threshold.

  * The interruption duration of a schema migration or full data synchronization task exceeds the specified threshold.

* Medium Protection: This protection level is applicable to scenarios with high requirements on latency. The threshold must be higher than or equal to the threshold configured for high protection.

* Low Protection: This protection level is applicable to scenarios with low requirements on latency. The threshold must be higher than or equal to that configured for medium protection.

The admin user can modify the **<UI-TERM>Alert Threshold</UI-TERM>** and **<UI-TERM>Alert Frequency</UI-TERM>** for each protection level. Regular users can mark the protection level for their own data synchronization tasks and view the **<UI-TERM>Alert Threshold</UI-TERM>** and **<UI-TERM>Alert Frequency</UI-TERM>** of each protection level.

## Alert settings

1. Log in to the OMS console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.utils.hooks.useLayoutMenus.SystemManagement">System Management</UI-TERM>** \> **<UI-TERM>Alert Management</UI-TERM>**.

3. On the **<UI-TERM>Alert Management</UI-TERM>** page, click the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Audit.constants.AlarmSettings">Alert Settings</UI-TERM>** tab.

4. On the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Audit.constants.AlarmSettings">Alert Settings</UI-TERM>** tab, configure the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.Card.AlertThreshold">Alert Threshold</UI-TERM>** and **<UI-TERM>Alert Frequency</UI-TERM>** parameters for **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.WarnLevel.HighProtection">High Protection</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.WarnLevel.MediumProtection">Medium Protection</UI-TERM>**, and **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.WarnLevel.DiBaoHu">Low Protection</UI-TERM>**.

   | Protection level  |        Parameter        |                                                                Description                                                                 |
   |-------------------|-------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
   | High protection   | Synchronization Latency | Value range: 1 second to 5 minutes.                                                                                                        |
   | High protection   | Interruption Duration   | Value range: 1 second to 5 minutes.                                                                                                        |
   | Medium protection | Synchronization Latency | Value range: 30 seconds to 10 minutes.                                                                                                     |
   | Medium protection | Interruption Duration   | Value range: 30 seconds to 10 minutes.                                                                                                     |
   | Low protection    | Synchronization Latency | Value range: 5 minutes to 3 hours.                                                                                                         |
   | Low protection    | Interruption Duration   | Value range: 5 minutes to 3 hours.                                                                                                         |
   | Alert Frequency                            || Value range: 1 minute to 600 minutes. The default value is 3 minutes. You can configure the frequency based on your business requirements. |

5. On the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.utils.hooks.useLayoutMenus.DataMigration">Data Migration</UI-TERM>** and **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.utils.hooks.useLayoutMenus.DataSynchronization">Data Synchronization</UI-TERM>** pages, view and modify the alert protection levels.

   For example, you can perform the following operations on the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.SystemParam.contants.DataMigration">Data Migration</UI-TERM>** page:

   1. In the left-side navigation pane, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.UserGuidance.DataMigration">Data Migration</UI-TERM>**.

   2. On the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.pages.Migration.MigrationProject">Migration Tasks</UI-TERM>**  page, click the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.SyncSwitch.DbObjectTable.MoreOperations">More</UI-TERM>** icon for the task whose alert protection level you want to modify, and then click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Migration.constants.ModifyAlertLevels">Modify Alert Level</UI-TERM>** in the drop-down list.

   3. In the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Sync.constants.ModifyAlertLevels">Modify Alert Level</UI-TERM>** dialog box, select the desired alert protection level.

   4. Click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.pages.Migration.TableRowCard.Submitted">Submit</UI-TERM>**.

## Create an alert channel

1. Log in to the OMS console. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="transmit.terminal.notification">System Management</UI-TERM>** \> **<UI-TERM>Alert MAnagement</UI-TERM>**.

2. On the **<UI-TERM>Alert Management</UI-TERM>** page, click the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Audit.constants.AlarmSettings">Alert Settings</UI-TERM>** tab.

3. In the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.AlarmChannel.AlertChannels">Alert Channels</UI-TERM>** section, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.AlarmChannelModal.CreateAnAlertChannel">Create Alert Channel</UI-TERM>** in the upper-right corner.

   ![Create](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/3674229461/p291330.png)

4. In the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.AlarmChannel.CreateAnAlertChannel">Create Alert Channel</UI-TERM>** dialog box, specify the following parameters.

   |         Parameter         |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
   |---------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
   | Channel Name              | The name of the input channel. The name must be 1 to 50 characters in length and can contain Chinese characters, English letters, digits, and underscores (_).                                  |
   | Channel Type              | Valid values: **<UI-TERM>OCP Alert</UI-TERM>** and **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.AlarmChannelModal.DingtalkAlarm">DingTalk Alerts</UI-TERM>**.                                                                                                                                          |
   | Configuration Information | Fill in the configuration information based on the selected channel type. <ul><li> If you select **<UI-TERM>OCP Alert</UI-TERM>**, select an OCP cluster from the associated OCP clusters.   <li> If you select **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.AlarmChannelModal.DingtalkAlarm">DingTalk Alerts</UI-TERM>**, you must specify the webhook access token of the DingTalk chatbot. For more information, see the "Obtain the access token of the DingTalk chatbot" section of this topic. <br> If you select DingTalk Alerts, you can specify a DingTalk group to receive the alerts.</ul>    |

5. Click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.pages.OCP.constant.TestConnection">Test Connection</UI-TERM>**.

6. After the test succeeds, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.OCP.components.OCPForm.Submitted">Submit</UI-TERM>**.

After you create an alert channel, you can edit or delete it.

* Edit an alert channel

  1. Click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Endpoint.constants.Editing">Edit</UI-TERM>** in the Actions column of the channel that you want to edit.

  2. In the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.AlarmChannelModal.ModifyAnAlertChannel">Modify Alert Channel</UI-TERM>** dialog box, modify the parameters.

     You can modify the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.AlarmChannelModal.ChannelName">Channel Name</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.AlarmChannelModal.ChannelType">Channel Type</UI-TERM>**, and **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.AlarmChannelModal.PeiZhiXinXi">Configuration Information</UI-TERM>** parameters.

  3. Click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.AlarmChannelModal.TestConnection">Test Connection</UI-TERM>**.

  4. After the test succeeds, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.JDBCWriter.New.Submitted">Submit</UI-TERM>**.

* Delete an alert channel

  1. Click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Migration.constants.Destruction">Delete</UI-TERM>** in the Actions column of the channel that you want to delete.

  2. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.EndpointPublic.constants.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>**.

## Obtain the access token of the DingTalk chatbot

1. Open the target DingTalk group and click the **<UI-TERM>Group Settings</UI-TERM>** icon in the upper-right corner.

2. Click **<UI-TERM>Group Assistant</UI-TERM>**.

3. In the **<UI-TERM>Group Assistant</UI-TERM>** panel, click **<UI-TERM>Add Robot</UI-TERM>**.

4. In the **<UI-TERM>ChatBot</UI-TERM>** dialog box, click the **+** icon.

5. Click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.pages.Audit.Custom">Custom</UI-TERM>** under the prompt **<UI-TERM>Please choose which robot to add</UI-TERM>**.

6. In the **<UI-TERM>Robot details</UI-TERM>** dialog box, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Store.New.Add">Add</UI-TERM>**.

7. In the **<UI-TERM>Add Robot</UI-TERM>** dialog box, specify the following parameters.

   |     Parameter     |                                                                                                                                                                         Description                                                                                                                                                                          |
   |-------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
   | Chatbot name      | The name of the custom chatbot.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
   | Add to Group      | The DingTalk group to which the chatbot is added. You cannot modify this parameter after you specify it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
   | Security Settings | Valid values: **<UI-TERM>Custom Keywords</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM>Additional Signature</UI-TERM>**, and **<UI-TERM>IP Address</UI-TERM>**. To ensure the security of the custom chatbot, select at least one security setting.  |

8. Select **I have read and accepted DingTalk Custom Robot Service Terms of Service** and click **<UI-TERM>Finished</UI-TERM>**.

9. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.FullValidation.UpdateValidatingSqlDrawer.Copy">Copy</UI-TERM>** to copy the webhook address. Obtain the string after the `access_token=` field. You can also directly copy the access token in the webhook address.

   **Notice**

   Keep the webhook address confidential. If the webhook address is disclosed, security risks may occur.

   ![Token information](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/3674229461/p392777.png)

10. Click **Finished**.
