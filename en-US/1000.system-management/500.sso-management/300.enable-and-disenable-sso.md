# Enable or disable an SSO integration

OceanBase Migration Service (OMS) Community Edition supports only one SSO integration. When multiple SSO integrations are created, you can enable or disable an SSO integration to switch the third-party login method. This topic describes how to enable or disable an SSO integration.

## Prerequisites

You have logged in to the OMS Community Edition console as an ADMIN user.

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OMS Community Edition console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="transmit.terminal.notification">System Management</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.pages.ExternalSSO.SSOManagement">SSO Management</UI-TERM>**.

3. On the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.pages.ExternalSSO.SSOManagement">SSO Management</UI-TERM>** page, click the switch in the **<UI-TERM key="ocp-v2.page.External.WhetherToEnable">Enable</UI-TERM>** column of the SSO integration that you want to enable or disable.

4. In the message that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Migration.hooks.useMigrationObject.Determine">OK</UI-TERM>**.
