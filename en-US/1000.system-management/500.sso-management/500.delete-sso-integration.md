# Delete an SSO integration

If the SSO integration that you have created is no longer suitable for your business scenario, delete it by following the steps in this topic.

## Prerequisites

The user logged in to the OMS Community Edition console is of the ADMIN role.

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OMS Community Edition console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="transmit.terminal.notification">System Management</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.pages.ExternalSSO.SSOManagement">SSO Management</UI-TERM>**.

3. On the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.pages.ExternalSSO.SSOManagement">SSO Management</UI-TERM>** page, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Sync.Detail.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.pages.EndpointPublic.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column of the SSO integration that you want to delete.

4. In the message that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Detail.components.UpdateSyncObj.Determine">OK</UI-TERM>**.
