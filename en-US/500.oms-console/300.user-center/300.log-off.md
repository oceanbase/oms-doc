# Log off

This topic describes how to log off the current user.

## Procedure

1. Log on to the console of OceanBase Migration Service (OMS) Community Edition.

2. In the upper-right corner of the OMS Community Edition console, click the username and choose **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.src.layout.BasicLayout.LogOut">Log Off</UI-TERM>**.

3. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Operator.Checker.useCheckerAction.Update">OK</UI-TERM>**.
