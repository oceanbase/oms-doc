# Configure user information

At User Center of the console of OceanBase Migration Service (OMS) Community Edition, you can configure your user information, such as your contact information and department.

## Procedure

1. Log on to the OMS Community Edition console.

2. In the upper-right corner of the OMS Community Edition console, click the username and choose **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.User.Personal.PersonalSettings">Personal Settings</UI-TERM>**.

   ![Personal Settings](https://help-static-aliyun-doc.aliyuncs.com/assets/img/en-US/4105718461/p397785.png)

3. On the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.src.layout.BasicLayout.PersonalSettings">Personal Settings</UI-TERM>** page, enter your information in the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.User.New.EmailAddressOptional">Email</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.User.New.MobilePhoneNumberOptional">Mobile Number</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.User.New.Department">Department</UI-TERM>**, and Description fields.

4. Click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Notification.Setting.AlarmChannelModal.Submitted">Submit</UI-TERM>**.
