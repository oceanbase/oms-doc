# Delete a store

This topic describes how to delete a store to save running resources.

## Limitations

* OceanBase Migration Service (OMS) Community Edition allows you to delete a store in the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Operator.constants.Stopped">Stopped</UI-TERM>**, **<UI-TERM>Exception</UI-TERM>**, or **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Migration.constants.UnexpectedExit">Unexpected Exit</UI-TERM>** state.

* After a store is deleted, it will be permanently deleted from the server and cannot be recovered. Proceed with caution.

## Procedure

1. Log in to the OMS Community Edition console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.SystemParam.contants.OMMonitoring">OPS & Monitoring</UI-TERM>** **>** **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Operation.OperationComponent.Component">Component</UI-TERM>**. The **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.MonitorDrawer.LogPullComponent">Store</UI-TERM>** tab automatically appears.

3. On the **<UI-TERM>Store</UI-TERM>** page, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.JDBCWriter.New.Delete">Delete</UI-TERM>** on the right of the target store, which must be in the **<UI-TERM>Exception</UI-TERM>** or **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Detail.components.SyncMonitorDrawer.Stopped">Stopped</UI-TERM>** state.

   You can filter stores by region and delete the stores that are abnormal or stopped in the target region.

4. In the dialog box that appears, enter **delete** and click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Operator.Connector.useConnectorAction.Update">OK</UI-TERM>**.
