# Rerun and resume a Full-Verification component

The OPS & Monitoring module of OceanBase Migration Service (OMS) Community Edition allows you to rerun and resume a Full-Verification component.

## Limitations

OMS Community Edition allows you to rerun and resume a Full-Verification component in the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Detail.components.SyncMonitorDrawer.Stopped">Stopped</UI-TERM>** or **<UI-TERM>Exception</UI-TERM>** state.

## Rerun a Full-Verification component

1. Log in to the OMS Community Edition console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.SystemParam.contants.OMMonitoring">OPS & Monitoring</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.utils.hooks.useLayoutMenus.Components">Component</UI-TERM>**.

3. On the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Operation.OperationComponent.Component">Component</UI-TERM>** page, click the **<UI-TERM>Full-Verification</UI-TERM>** tab.

4. On the Full-Verification page, you can search for a Full-Verification component by component name, Migration Task Name, or server IP address.

   You can filter Full-Verification components by region and rerun the Full-Verification components in the target region.

5. Click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Migration.constants.Rerun">Rerun</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.MonitorDrawer.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column of the target Full-Verification component.

6. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Operator.Connector.useConnectorAction.Update">OK</UI-TERM>**.

## Resume a Full-Verification component

1. Log in to the OMS Community Edition console.

2. In the left-side navigation pane, choose **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Welcome.constants.OMMonitoring">OPS & Monitoring</UI-TERM>** > **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.utils.hooks.useLayoutMenus.Components">Component</UI-TERM>**.

3. On the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Audit.constants.Component">Component</UI-TERM>** page, click the **<UI-TERM>Full-Verification</UI-TERM>** tab.

4. On the Full-Verification page, you can search for a Full-Verification component by component name, Migration Task Name, or server IP address.

   You can filter Full-Verification components by region and resume the Full-Verification components in the target region.

5. Click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.Operation.constants.Recovery">Resume</UI-TERM>** in the **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.MonitorDrawer.Operation">Actions</UI-TERM>** column of the target Full-Verification component.

6. In the dialog box that appears, click **<UI-TERM key="oms-community.components.TreeTransfer.TargetNode.Determine">OK</UI-TERM>**.
